## 🛠️ Practical Filming & Video Production Tips

### 1. Pre-Recording Setup & Hotkeys
* **Dual-Window Setup**: Keep PPT in Fullscreen Presentation Mode and Chrome Browser open at `http://localhost:3000` on Central Registry. Use **`Alt + Tab`** once smoothly at `00:50` to switch without fumbling.
* **Screen Resolution**: Set display resolution to standard **1920x1080 (1080p)** with 100% DPI scaling (not 125% or 150%) so fonts and cards fit crisply.
* **Clean Browser Chrome**: Press **`F11`** for Fullscreen Browser mode to hide browser bookmarks, URL bar, and extensions for a 100% native government portal look.

### 2. Audio & Pacing Guidance
* **Word Count**: The script contains ~420 spoken words. At a natural presenter speed of 120–130 words per minute, this takes **exactly 3 minutes and 20 seconds**, leaving 10 seconds of comfortable transition buffer!
* **Tone**: Do not sound like a student reading a text. Speak with the executive authority of an e-Governance Product Architect.
* **Microphone**: Keep the mic 15–20 cm away; avoid fan or AC noise.

### 3. Cursor Movements (Camera Discipline)
* **No Jitter**: Never wiggle or circle the cursor violently. Move it in straight, purposeful lines to each button.
* **Hover and Rest**: When pointing to an important badge (like the DPDP token or the Conflict Alert), hover the cursor and let it rest for 1.5 seconds so the evaluator's eyes focus on it.

### 4. Evaluator Q&A Preparedness (Defensive Traps)
* **Q: "Is this only for Tamil Nadu colleges?"**  
  * **Answer**: *"No. Our entity resolver and registry database support all 10,800+ AICTE-approved institutions across India using canonical AICTE Permanent IDs."*
* **Q: "Does the AI overrule human officers?"**
