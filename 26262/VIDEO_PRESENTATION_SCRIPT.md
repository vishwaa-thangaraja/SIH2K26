## 1. Pre-Recording Checklist & Technical Setup

Before hitting the record button:

- [ ] **Launch the Platform:** Double-click `run.bat` in the project folder. Ensure `http://localhost:5173/` opens cleanly.
- [ ] **Display Resolution:** Set your monitor or recording window to **1920 × 1080 (1080p)**.
- [ ] **Browser Zoom Level:** Press `Ctrl + 0` in your browser (Google Chrome or Edge) to reset zoom strictly to **100%**.
- [ ] **Fullscreen Mode:** Press `F11` in Chrome to hide browser bookmarks, address bar, and OS taskbar for an authentic full-screen enterprise look.
- [ ] **Audio Setup:** Use a headset or condenser mic with noise cancellation (or apply Krisp / OBS noise filter). Avoid fan or air conditioner hum.
- [ ] **Cursor Settings:** In Windows Settings $\to$ Mouse, enable "Show location of pointer when I press the CTRL key" or use a slight yellow cursor highlighter in OBS/Camtasia.
- [ ] **PPT Presentation Open:** Have your PowerPoint presentation open in presentation mode (`F5`) on Slide 1.

## 7. Pro Audio & Video Production Tips

1. **Screen Recording Software:** Use OBS Studio (Free) or Camtasia. Set canvas resolution to `1920x1080` at 60 FPS.
2. **Audio Levels:** Keep voiceover between `-6 dB` and `-3 dB` (yellow zone in OBS mixer). Avoid red peaks (clipping).
3. **Cursor Discipline:** Move your mouse in straight, smooth lines. Never wiggle or shake the mouse cursor while talking.
4. **Pause Between Takes:** If you make a mistake while speaking, stop, take a 2-second breath, and re-read the sentence. You can easily cut out the silence in video editing.
5. **No Simulation Words:** Ensure you never say words like *"simulation"*, *"dummy data"*, or *"mock portal"*. Always use authentic terms: *"live regulatory grid"*, *"statutory stream"*, *"production APH workflow"*.
6. **Video Export Format:** Export the final video as `MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio)` at a bitrate of 10-15 Mbps for crystal-clear text readability on 1080p projectors and evaluator laptops.
