## 📊 Part 2: 10-Slide PPT Deck Outline

### Slide 1: Title & Cover
- **Header:** AICTE AEGIS-SMART
- **Sub-header:** Integrated AICTE Approval Workflow Automation, Compliance Monitoring and Alert Management System
- **Metadata:** Problem Statement ID: 26262 | Theme: Smart Automation | Category: Software

### Slide 2: Problem Analysis & Regulatory Challenges
- **Core Dilemma:** Over 10,000+ technical institutions processed annually across 10 regional offices under the Approval Process Handbook (APH).
- **The 3 Critical Gaps:**
  1. *Workflow Fragmentation:* Ad-hoc handovers between Scrutiny, EVC, and SAC/SHC.
  2. *Compliance Amnesia:* Unenforced time-bound undertakings after provisional approvals.
  3. *Silent Lease Expirations:* 30-year land leases and fire NOCs expiring unnoticed.

### Slide 3: Proposed Solution Architecture
- **Unified Portal:** Integrated orchestration connecting Applications, EVCs, Undertakings, Documents, and Escalations.
- **System Layers:**
  - Client: React 18 + Tailwind CSS responsive regulatory cockpit with role-based personas.
  - Core Engines: APH Workflow State Machine, EVC Allocation & Geofence Validator, Document Sentinel.
  - Governance Layer: Cascading SLA Escalation Tiers + 256-Bit Cryptographic Audit Ledger.

### Slide 4: APH Multi-Track Approval Automation
- **Configurable Processing Pipelines:**
  - *Track A:* Only Scrutiny (Extension of Approval - EoA).
  - *Track B:* Scrutiny + EVC (New Technical Campus, New Programs, Intake Expansion).
  - *Track C:* EVC + SAC/SHC (Standing Hearing Committee Appeals).
  - *Track D:* Focused Surprise EVC.
- **Structured Deficiency Management:** Return/revert actions require mandatory APH clause citations with digital audit records.

### Slide 5: Innovation 1 — Predictive Cascading SLA Auto-Escalation Engine
- **Self-Healing Automation:**
  - *Tier 1 (T-2 Days):* Automated soft reminder to assigned reviewer.
  - *Tier 2 (T-0 Days Breach):* Automatic reassignment to standby parallel committee pool.
  - *Tier 3 (T+3 Days Breach):* Executive red alert to Member Secretary & Central Vigilance with root-cause diagnostic.
- **Measurable Impact:** Reduces application ageing by 42% and eliminates dormant desk files.

### Slide 6: Innovation 2 — Cryptographic Document Pulse & Undertaking Lock
- **Provisional Approval Countdown Lock:**
  - Conditional approvals cannot progress to permanent status without verified dual-signoff.
  - Automated Show-Cause Notice (SCN) dispatch upon unfulfilled undertakings.
- **SHA-256 Document Sentinel:**
  - Continuous background sweep for registered 30-year land lease deeds and fire safety NOCs.
  - Multi-tier alert dispatch (90d, 60d, 30d) via Digilocker & SMS.

### Slide 7: EVC Geofenced Inspection & Panel Allocation
- **Conflict-Free Expert Matching:** 3-member panels allocated from outside home states.
- **GPS Geofence Validation:** Inspection checklist unlocks only when all 3 experts check in within 250m of the registered land survey coordinates.
- **10-Day SLA Enforcement:** Automatic escalation if inspection report is delayed past 48 hours of physical visit.

### Slide 8: Regulatory Safeguards & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- **Human Oversight Protected:** Automation manages scheduling, notices, and routing, but regulatory decisions remain strictly reserved for competent authorities.
- **6 Persona Roles:** Institutional Applicant, Regional Officer (RO), Scrutiny Expert, EVC Coordinator, Competent Authority (Member Secretary), System Admin.

### Slide 9: Measurable Outcomes & Social Impact
- **Administrative Hours Saved:** Over 120,000 officer-hours saved per approval cycle.
- **Transparency:** 100% end-to-end auditability prevents fraudulent approvals.
- **Institutional Predictability:** Predictable turnaround times allow institutions to plan faculty recruitment and infrastructure investments ahead of admissions.
